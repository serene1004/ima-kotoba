## Design Context

### Users
Korean Japanese learners who want to capture their current feeling or thought in one short Japanese sentence. The primary use is a quick mobile check-in, not long-form journaling.

### Brand Personality
Quiet, warm, encouraging. The product name is **いまことば (Ima Kotoba)** and its promise is: "지금의 마음을 일본어로 기록해요."

### Aesthetic Direction
Mobile-first personal diary. Use calm paper-like neutrals, a restrained indigo accent, expressive but small emotion marks, and generous whitespace. Avoid a productivity-dashboard or social-media feel.

### Design Principles
1. Make writing one sentence feel effortless.
2. Let today’s unfinished thought be the clear next action.
3. Keep Japanese text visually primary and Korean supporting text secondary.
4. Show history as a gentle personal timeline.
5. Preserve accessible contrast, visible focus, and touch-friendly controls.
